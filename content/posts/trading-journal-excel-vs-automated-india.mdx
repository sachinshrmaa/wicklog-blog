## Where Excel Trading Journals Break for Indian Traders

**Spreadsheets break on five specific things: fills vs trades, partial exits, Indian charges, F&O symbols and which month a P&L belongs to.** None of these show up in week one, which is why the template looks fine at first.

The charges row deserves a closer look. Budget 2026 raised STT on option premium sales from 0.10% to 0.15% and on futures sales from 0.02% to 0.05%, effective 1 April 2026. Every spreadsheet with hard-coded STT formulas has been quietly overstating net P&L since then, unless someone updated it.

## Common Mistakes & Troubleshooting

**Most import problems come from edited files, the wrong report, or overlapping date ranges.** Here are the fixes.

### "The import didn't recognise my file"

The importer identifies the format from the header row. If you renamed, deleted or reordered columns, or opened and re-saved the file in a way that changed headers, it falls back to the WickLog template format. Re-download the original tradebook and import it unedited.

### "I imported the P&L statement and got strange trades"

Broker P&L statements are summaries, not executions. Use the tradebook report.

### "Will importing the same month twice create duplicates?"

No. Each imported fill gets a key, and re-importing an overlapping tradebook skips fills already imported. You can safely export "last 3 months" every month.

### "My open positions show an estimated entry date"

Open positions created from broker holdings or position snapshots don't always include the original buy date. WickLog flags them "entry date estimated" until the opening fill is found or you confirm the date.

### "My sells closed the wrong lot"

Matching is FIFO per symbol and product type, so intraday (MIS) trades never close delivery (CNC) holdings. If you hold the same stock in both, check the product type on the original trades.

### "Net P&L doesn't match my contract note exactly"

Small differences usually come from rounding or from options that were exercised at expiry, which attract STT on settlement value. Mark those trades with the "options exercised" flag so STT is calculated on the right basis.
