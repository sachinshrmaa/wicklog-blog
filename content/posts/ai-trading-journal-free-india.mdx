## The Root Problem: Your Mistakes Hide in Data You Never Re-Read

**Traders repeat the same mistakes because the evidence is spread across hundreds of trades and no one reads them back.** A P&L screen shows today's result. It does not show that 9 of your last 12 red days started with a loss before 10 AM, followed by four quick re-entries.

Three things keep these patterns invisible:

- **Volume.** An active options trader logs 150–300 trades a month. Reviewing them by hand is a weekend job nobody does twice.
- **Gross vs net.** Kite's P&L view and most spreadsheets are read in gross terms. The leak often shows up only after STT (0.15% on option premium sales since April 2026), brokerage and GST.
- **Memory bias.** You remember the big winner and the big loser. The ₹600 loss you took four times on a Tuesday afternoon doesn't register, even when it adds up to more than either.

An AI journal earns its place by doing the re-reading for you and answering in numbers.

## Seven Questions That Find Real Leaks in an Indian F&O Account

**The best AI journal prompts ask for a comparison in rupees, after charges, over a defined period.** Vague prompts ("how am I doing?") get vague answers.

These are written for Ticker AI, but they work as a review checklist with any journal.

### 1. "Compare my net P&L on trades taken within 30 minutes of a losing trade vs all other trades, last 60 days."

This is the closest data proxy for revenge trading. WickLog does not tag trades with emotions; it doesn't need to. Entry timestamps after a loss tell the story.

### 2. "Break down my win rate and net P&L by entry hour for Nifty options."

Morning momentum and the 12:00–1:30 PM range behave very differently. Many intraday traders find their first hour pays for their afternoon. The free **Win rate by hour** dashboard widget shows this too; the AI lets you slice it by instrument or setup.

### 3. "Which strategy tag has the lowest profit factor after charges?"

A setup that wins 60% of the time can still lose money if its losers are twice the size of its winners. Profit factor (gross wins ÷ gross losses) after charges is the honest ranking.

### 4. "How did I do on Nifty expiry Tuesdays vs other days this quarter?"

Expiry-day option premiums decay fast, and many traders size up for the "cheap lottery" trade. Find out whether yours pay.

### 5. "What share of my monthly gross profit went to charges in each of the last three months?"

If this number is rising, you are trading more for the same edge. Since the April 2026 STT revision, many option scalpers have seen it jump.

### 6. "List my five largest losses and what my journal entries said that day."

This links outcomes to context. If your journal says "missed the morning move, chasing" on three of your five worst days, that is the pattern.

### 7. "Which symbols have I traded more than 10 times with negative net P&L?"

The repeated losing symbol is one of the most common leaks. WickLog's free **Mistake pattern detector** widget flags it automatically from closed trades.
