> **TL;DR:** Trading expectancy tells you the average ₹ you can expect to make (or lose) per trade over the long run. Formula: `(Win% × Avg Win) − (Loss% × Avg Loss)`. A positive number means your system has an edge — a high win rate with negative expectancy is still a losing strategy.

You can win 7 out of 10 trades this month and still be bleeding money. Most retail traders in NSE F&O chase win rate because it feels good — green trades feel like validation. But win rate alone tells you nothing about whether your strategy survives the next 100 trades. Expectancy does.

## The Root Problem

Retail traders obsess over accuracy because it's the number that shows up loudest in their head after every trade. A 70% win rate *sounds* like a great system. But if your average loss is 3x your average win, that system is mathematically designed to drain your account.

Here's the math nobody shows you in trading Telegram groups: expectancy is the single number that combines how often you win with how much you win or lose when you do. It's the difference between "I feel like I'm doing well" and "I have actual proof my edge exists."

Most Indian retail traders never calculate this because it requires two things they don't have: a large enough trade sample, and every trade logged with entry, exit, and P&L in one place — not scattered across a Kite contract note, a Groww statement, and a mental tally of "gut feel" wins.
