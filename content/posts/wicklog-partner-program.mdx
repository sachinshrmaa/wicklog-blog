Trading is often a solitary journey — hours spent alone with charts, journals, and your own discipline. But the traders who grow the fastest are rarely the ones going it alone. They're in Telegram groups at 9 AM sharing setups, in Discord servers debriefing after market close, swapping journal screenshots with their trading buddies.

We built Wicklog because journaling should be effortless. Today, we're building on that with something new: a way to reward the traders who've already been telling their friends about us.

![WickLog Partner Program](/blog/images/wl-partner-program.png)

**Say hello to the Wicklog Partner Program** — our referral system for the users who believe in disciplined trading enough to bring their community along. For every friend you refer who upgrades to a Pro plan, you earn real cash, credited straight to your wallet and payable to your bank account.

## How It Works

We kept this simple — three steps, no fine print gymnastics.

**1. Share your unique link**
Head to your Wicklog Dashboard and grab your personalized referral link or code. Drop it in your Telegram groups, Discord servers, on Twitter, or just send it directly to the trading buddy who keeps asking what journal you use.

**2. Your friend upgrades to Pro**
Your friend signs up, gets a feel for the platform, and decides to get serious about their journaling by upgrading to any Wicklog Pro plan — monthly or annual, doesn't matter.

**3. You get rewarded, instantly**
The moment that upgrade goes through, ₹150 lands in your Wicklog wallet. No waiting around, no manual approval queue.

## The Details

We wanted this to be as transparent as everything else we build.

- **The reward:** ₹150 per successful Pro conversion, with no upper limit. Refer 5 friends or 50 — every single one pays out.
- **Minimum payout:** Once your wallet hits ₹500, you can request a withdrawal.
- **Payment methods:** Choose UPI for instant transfers, or Bank Transfer (IMPS) if you prefer.
- **Processing time:** Payouts are reviewed and processed within 24–48 hours, with email notifications at every step — request received, under review, and paid out.

## Why We Built This

Wicklog didn't grow through ad spend. It grew because traders told other traders. Someone posted a screenshot of their equity curve in a group chat, someone else asked "what journal is that," and the answer was always the same.

We'd rather put that ad budget directly into the pockets of the people already doing the work of spreading the word. Your success bringing friends onto a proper journaling habit is, quite literally, our success too — and this program is our way of making that explicit instead of just implicit.

More than the ₹150, though, this is about something we care about more: helping the traders in your circle build the kind of discipline that actually moves the needle. A referral to Wicklog isn't just a link — it's a nudge toward a friend logging their trades, reviewing their mistakes, and trading with more intention than before.
