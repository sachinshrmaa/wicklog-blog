## What Is the Wicklog + Zerodha Kite Integration?

Wicklog is a trading journal built for Indian traders. The Kite integration connects your Zerodha account to Wicklog using the official *Zerodha Kite Connect API* — so your trades are fetched on-demand, with zero manual data entry.

Once connected, you can:

- *Fetch all your trades* for any day range in your Wicklog account
- *Write journal entries* on top of each trade — your reasoning, emotions, mistakes, lessons
- *Attach chart screenshots* of your setups to each trade
- *See your trading stats* — win rate, net P&L, average risk-reward, best and worst instruments

## Step-by-Step

### Step 1: Go to Connect Demat

From the Wicklog sidebar, click *Connect Demat*. You'll see three broker cards — Dhan, Zerodha Kite, and Groww — each showing a "Not Connected" status. This screen also lays out the read-only guarantee up front: Wicklog can pull your trade history, but it cannot place or modify orders.

![How to Connect Zerodha Kite to Wicklog](/wicklog-blog/public/images/connect-demat-dashboard.png)

![How to Connect Zerodha Kite to Wicklog](/wicklog-blog/public/images/connect-demat-2.png)

### Step 2: Authenticate with Kite

Click *Connect* on the Zerodha Kite card. This redirects you to Kite's own login screen (not a Wicklog-branded page) — that's the point. You enter your Kite user ID, password, and TOTP directly with Zerodha, using their official OAuth flow. Wicklog never sees or stores your Kite password.

On success, Kite redirects you back to Wicklog's Trades screen, and the sidebar now shows "Import from Zerodha Kite" as an active option.

One thing worth knowing: this Kite session *expires periodically*, so you'll need to reconnect every so often rather than staying linked forever. That's a deliberate tradeoff — a session that expires is a session with a much smaller window for anything to go wrong, so it works in your favor even though it means one extra click occasionally.
![How to Connect Zerodha Kite to Wicklog](/wicklog-blog/public/images/login-to-kite.png)

### Step 3: Import your trades

Click *Import from Zerodha Kite* at the top right of the Trades screen. A popup opens asking you to choose:

- *Trade Type* — for example, Open Trades Only
- *From Date* and *To Date* — the window you want pulled in

Set your range and click *Import Trades*. Wicklog fetches your trade history for that window in one shot — no CSV exports, no manual entry.

### Step 4: See it reflected everywhere

Once the import finishes, your trades appear directly in the Trades table with live status. In one real import, Wicklog pulled in three open positions — SILVERBEES, NIFTYBEES, and GOLDBEES — each showing entry price, live LTP, quantity, and running P&L (₹28,936.90 current value against ₹30,712.10 invested, updating live with a visible "Live" indicator and timestamp).

From here, the same trade data automatically feeds:

- *Dashboard* — your overall trading statistics and performance
- *Journals* — write entries and link them directly to the imported trades
- *Ticker AI* — ask questions about your own trading activity and get answers grounded in your actual history

## How Secure Is the Kite Integration?

Security is something we take seriously at Wicklog. Here's exactly how it works.

### Read-Only Access — Always

When you authorize Wicklog, you grant *read-only* permission. Wicklog can *only read* your trade history. It *cannot*:

- Place, modify, or cancel orders
- Access your funds or holdings for transactions
- Transfer money in any form

This is enforced at the API level by Zerodha — not just a promise we make.

### Your Kite Password Never Touches Wicklog

Authorization happens entirely on Zerodha's own servers. You log in directly on Zerodha's page — Wicklog never sees your username, password, or TOTP. Wicklog only receives a *short-lived access token* issued by Zerodha after you authorize.

> *Note:* Kite Connect access tokens expire daily at *6 AM IST*. You'll need to reconnect each morning to import new trades.

This is the same OAuth 2.0 standard used by banks and major financial apps.

### Revoke Access Any Time

You are always in control. To disconnect:

1. Go to Zerodha Kite → *Apps & Permissions*
2. Find Wicklog and click *Revoke Access*

Or disconnect directly from *Wicklog → Settings → Brokers → Disconnect*.

Once revoked, Wicklog loses all access to your Kite data immediately.

## Common Mistakes / Troubleshooting

- *"Connect" button doesn't seem to do anything* — check for a blocked popup; the Kite login opens in a redirect and some browsers silently block it on first load.
- *Import shows fewer trades than expected* — check your *From Date*; the default range may not go back far enough to cover older trades. Widen the date range and re-import.
- *Asked to log in again after a few days* — this is expected. Kite sessions expire on a schedule for security; just click Connect again to refresh it.
- *TOTP rejected* — make sure you're entering the live 6-digit code from your authenticator app, not your static Kite PIN — these are commonly mixed up on first connect.

## FAQ

*Is Zerodha Kite import available on the free plan?*
Import from Zerodha Kite requires a Wicklog Pro account — the Connect Demat screen is accessible under Pro.

*Does Wicklog have write access to my Kite account?*
No. Wicklog uses Kite's OAuth login and only reads your tradebook. It cannot place, modify, or cancel orders — see Wicklog's data accuracy limitations page for more detail on what's pulled.

*Why does my Kite connection need to be renewed?*
Kite sessions are time-limited by design. Reconnecting periodically, instead of staying permanently linked, is what keeps the data-leak surface small if a session token were ever compromised.

*Can I import only specific trades instead of everything?*
Yes — the Import Options popup lets you filter by Trade Type (e.g., Open Trades Only) and set a custom From/To date range before importing.
